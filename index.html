How long do you want to make art for?

Today an artist runs their career across a dozen broken tools: one app for exposure, one to host a portfolio, one to ask for money, another to find gigs, a doc for the resume, a separate thread to get feedback. greenchip replaces that fragmented toolkit with a singular platform where dialogue, presentation, income, and opportunity coalesce.

Only a fraction of artists make a living from their art practice due to how the system functions today. greenchip is here to nurture community and challenge the idea of what a lasting art career looks like.
